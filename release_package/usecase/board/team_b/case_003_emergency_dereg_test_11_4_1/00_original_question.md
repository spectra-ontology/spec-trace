## Question body (meaning preserved verbatim from the original)

3GPP 38.523-1, Correction of emergency services test cases 11.4.1 UE may perform de-registration procedure after emergency call is released at step 3A of emergency services test case 11.4.1. Currently this is not handled. TS 24.501, clause 5.5.2.1: When upper layers indicate that emergency services are no longer required, the UE if still registered for emergency services, may perform UE-initiated de-registration procedure followed by a re-registration to regain normal services, if the UE is in or moves to a suitable cell. 11.4.1.3.2 Test procedure sequence Table 11.4.1.3.2-1: Main behaviour 3B SS starts timer T1 = 5 seconds. 3BA Timer T1=5 seconds expires. 3EA The SS transmits an RRCRelease message. <-- NR RRC: RRCRelease - - 3EB SS configures the RA Response. - - - - 3F – 9A2 Void - - - - 9A3 – 9A21a1 Steps 2-20a1 of Table 4.5.2.2-2: Registration procedure for initial registration as specified in TS 38.508-1 [4] are performed on NR Cell 1. - - 2 P The content above was added; for this RAN5 CR we need to confirm whether the corresponding content exists in 3GPP 24.501 and 23.501. We also need the background and discussion history behind introducing R5-253194. The 24.501 clause cited in the CR says that when the upper layers no longer require the emergency call, the UE may perform De-registration in order to regain normal service. That behaviour is optional, and it is not clearly specified when the de-registration may be performed. Q1) On what basis is the 5-second value in step 3B set? Q2) In step 3BA added by R5-253194, the UE cannot perform De-registration until the 5 seconds elapse, and only afterwards — after receiving the RRC Release in Step 3EA — must it perform De-registration; we need the reason for this. Q3) If there were no RRC Release at Step 3EA, we would like to know whether the UE should not perform De-registration and should remain registered for Emergnecy service.
