## Question body (content preserved verbatim in meaning)

[OPEN] Background for the removal of the PC1.5-related wording in v17

This is a question that arose during mutual verification work with developers in the IODT (Inter-Operative Development Test) part.

Q: For UE power class 1.5 the maximum output power for single-port transmission is defined as the sum of the maximum output power from both UE antenna connectors. For PUSCH tranmissions, a UEs supporting PC1.5 shall meet the maximum output power requirement when scheduled by DCI format 0_0 or by DCI format 0_1 configured for single antenna port. This wording was present in 38.101 v16 but disappeared in v17. Why was it removed? Was there a meeting, or is there a specific reason?
