Relationship definitions (schema/validation discussion)

Proposed clarification:
REFERENCES records a document citation in a reported decision context. Its
interpretation may be scoped agreement, discussion basis, non-pursuit, or competing
proposals. MADE_AT denotes reported meeting provenance. RESOLUTION_BELONGS_TO
requires agenda-scope interpretation beyond a heading candidate. MODIFIES_SECTION
denotes the affected-clause declaration on a retained CR cover, at its explicit
release, and SENT_TO denotes an explicitly addressed recipient in a retained
liaison register. Observing WG and sending WG are distinct.
