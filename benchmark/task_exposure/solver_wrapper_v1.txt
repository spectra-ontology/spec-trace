SPECTRA solver instructions v1

Answer within the specified working group's frozen record snapshot. Return the
complete set of distinct rows in the requested field order. Use recorded
identifiers and values. Treat document and pack identifiers as exact, and
qualify a CR number by its specification. A zero-row answer means the
qualifying record set in this snapshot is empty.

A qualifying disposition has source verification, applies to the whole target
document, and has a recorded working-group decision meeting. Join each qualifying
disposition and meeting to each applicable recorded pack and plenary meeting
when requested. Resolve pack membership by exact document ID.

Apply these rules to the corresponding requested operation:
- Pack lookup returns all packs that contain the identified document, with
  their plenary meetings.
- Pack origin returns all recorded members of the named pack that have a
  qualifying disposition, with their working-group decision meetings.
- Decision-to-pack tracing returns every qualifying disposition/decision
  meeting crossed with every pack/plenary meeting for that document.
- Decision-to-clause tracing adds every clause label explicitly represented by
  a stored section-change link from that document. Project the section link to
  its clause label across all stored editions and deduplicate the resulting
  rows.
- Revision tracing follows all forward revision links transitively. Include
  every reachable later document with a qualifying disposition and pack,
  including qualifying intermediate revisions.
- Clause history identifies the exact specification, release and clause.
  Children are separate section IDs. Require that a changing document's target
  release matches the section release. Return each qualifying disposition,
  working-group decision meeting and requested pack evidence.
- Clause neighbours share an exact section and target release with the
  anchor document. Return all other matching documents with qualifying
  dispositions and their requested meeting and pack evidence.
- First change examines each exact section touched by a qualifying member of
  the anchor pack in its target release. Keep the section when no document
  changing it in that release belongs to a pack at a smaller plenary meeting
  number. Earlier changers are eligible by their recorded pack membership.
- Previous pack uses the same exact-section and release criteria. For each
  touched section with an earlier pack, return all packs tied at the largest
  plenary meeting number strictly below the anchor's. Earlier changers are
  eligible by their recorded pack membership.

The task below specifies the authorized interface and output format. Follow
the supplied schema for requested queries, and make a zero-row answer
executable when a query is requested.
